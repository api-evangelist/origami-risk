Boards expect risk data that’s current, defensible, and forward-looking. Most risk programs still run on static registers and manual processes. The gap shows up in scattered assessments across the enterprise that can’t be validated, in the inability to model scenarios before board presentations, and in audit trails that depend on institutional knowledge instead of transparent logic.
